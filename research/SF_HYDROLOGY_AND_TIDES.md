# San Francisco Urban Hydrology, Buried Creeks & November 2026 King Tides

> **Core Thesis for Judges:** San Francisco floods in the **exact same blocks every storm** because 19th-century builders paved over historic creeks, tidal lagoons, and bay marshes into a gravity-drained **combined sewer system**. ## 1. San Francisco's Buried Creeks & Filled Bay Margins
*Sources: San Francisco Estuary Institute (SFEI), US Coast Survey T-sheets, SFPUC SSIP engineering plans.*

1. **Mission Creek & Mission Bay Fill (`17th & Folsom`, `SoMa`, `4th & King / Caltrain` — `/ride` destination & `r50_c66`):** Historic tidal estuary & creek filled with 1850s/1906 rubble; bowl-shaped valley with chronic manhole geysers (SFPUC *Folsom Area Stormwater Improvement Project*).
2. **Hayes Creek (`Civic Center`, `Grove & Van Ness`, `Market St / UN Plaza` — `/ride` v2 watch block `r52_c52`):** Buried perennial stream ("Ghost Arroyo") from Alamo Square under Civic Center; groundwater literally feeds the **UN Plaza fountain** and forces **24/7 dewatering pumps at BART Civic Center & Powell stations**.
3. **Islais Creek (`Lower Alemany Blvd`, `Bayshore & Industrial` [Nov 18 Waymo stall!], `Toland / Illinois`):** **SF's largest historic drainage basin** (~1/3 of SF) funneled through buried culverts into flat Bay fill (SFPUC *Lower Alemany 10-ft tunnel project*).
4. **Washerwoman's Lagoon & Marina Fill (`Marina Blvd`, `Lombard`, `Laguna`, `Polk`):** Former tidal lagoon (*Laguna Pequeña*) filled with loose sand & 1906 debris sitting just above Bay high-tide elevation.

## 2. San Francisco's Combined Sewer System (SFPUC)

- **Only coastal city in California with a combined sewer system:** ~1,000 miles of shared sewage + stormwater pipes (~70 MGD dry weather, up to 575 MGD wet-weather treatment + ~200M gal storage boxes).
- **Design Storm Ceiling:** Modeled on a **5-year, 3-hour design storm (~1.3 in over 3 hrs ≈ 0.43 in/hr)**; localized stress begins at **0.25–0.5 in/hr**. Nov 18's **2.64 in** (~2.11" before noon) was **2× the design storm**.
- **Tidal Impedance & Manhole Geysers:** Gravity-fed via **~36 Combined Sewer Discharge (CSD) outfalls (29 on the Bayside)**. High tides submerge outfall gates (**tailwater effect**), spiking the Hydraulic Grade Line (HGL) above street grade and blowing manhole covers off streets.
