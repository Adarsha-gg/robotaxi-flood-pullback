How it works

A robotaxi ride on Nov 18, the day Waymo paused San Francisco.

Before pickup and while driving, it checks the streets ahead against the forecast, flood history and lane closures. When something looks risky, Genie 4 simulates that street at the moment the car would arrive, and Gemini 4 Carbon decides. No human in the loop.

Data: SF 311 street-flood reports · SFMTA permitted closures · Open-Meteo forecasts · Caltrans cameras. Models: Genie 4, Decision 1, Gemini 4 Carbon. Simulated scenes are illustrations seeded with real images.
